Behind this demo

This site is a live demo — here's what you get when you order one

What you're looking at is a working demo of a salon site. Order one for your business and you don't just get the pages — you get a small CRM that runs on your own Google account: contacts, a calendar for every person on the team, and emails that send themselves.

Get a live demo by email→

What you get

A website — plus the system behind it

01

Google-CRM for contacts & leads

Every booking and enquiry lands in your own Google Sheet — name, phone, service, source. No new app to learn, no monthly subscription. It's your spreadsheet.

02

A calendar for every team member

Each booking becomes an event in the right person's Google Calendar, with a reminder before the visit. Your team already lives in Google Calendar — now the bookings do too.

03

Emails that send themselves

Confirmations, reminders, thank-yous and win-backs — in Hebrew, Russian or English, matched to each client. Branded to your salon, sent automatically.

04

A pack of branded slides

A short, branded deck about your offer — ready to email a client or just show on screen. Built from your own data, in your client's language.

How it works

Three steps, no servers to babysit

  1. 1

    A client books online

    They pick a service, a master and a time — 24/7, in their own language.

  2. 2

    It lands in your Google account

    A row in your CRM sheet, an event in the master's calendar — instantly, with nothing to install.

  3. 3

    The follow-up runs itself

    Confirmation and reminder go out automatically; every morning you get the day's agenda by email.

Why Google

Your data, your account, no lock-in

The whole system lives inside a Google account you own — Sheets, Calendar, Gmail. Nothing to host, nothing to maintain, and if we ever part ways the data stays with you. It works the same in Hebrew, Russian and English.
